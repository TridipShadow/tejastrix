USES
  • Promotes wound healing.
  • Moisturizes and hydrates the skin.
  • Contains antioxidants and antibacterial properties.
  • Helps treat acne and pimples.
  • Reduces dandruff and conditions hair.
CULTIVATION
  • Soil: Well-drained sandy or loamy soil is ideal.
  • Climate: Grows best in hot, dry conditions (25°C - 40°C).
  • Watering: Water sparingly; allow the soil to dry between watering.
  • Propagation: Use root suckers or offsets for planting.
  • Harvesting: Ready for harvest 8-10 months after planting.
PARTS USED
  • The primary parts used are the leaves.
  • The gel, extracted from the inner leaf pulp, is used in topical applications.
  • The latex, derived from the outer leaf skin, is used for its laxative properties.
ACTIVE COMPOUNDS
  • Aloe Vera contains polysaccharides like acemannan, which provide its healing properties.
  • Other active compounds include vitamins A, C, and E.
  • Enzymes, amino acids, and minerals contribute to its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects.
